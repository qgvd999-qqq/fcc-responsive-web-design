Bread Recipe

Doughy Melt in the Mouth Bread

Ingredients

  • 4 Cups Flour
  • 1 1/4 Bakers Yeast
  • 2 Cups of warm water more as needed
  • 4 sticks Salted Butter
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • Parchment Paper
  • Loaf Pan

Instructions

  1. Mix Yeast, warm water, and butter. Once yeast begins to foam it's ready for next ingredient.
  2. Add Salt.
  3. Lightly dust Flour on parchment paper.
  4. Mix Flour with yeast mixture slowly until forms loaf.
  5. Dust Loaf pan with flour
